Thank you for using Ashmere (formerly Philosophize). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our iPhone and Android app. We keep your data minimal and secure.

Account registration is optional. You can use Ashmere without creating an account. If you register, you gain saved progress and cross-device sync. By downloading or using Ashmere, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Guest Users (No Account)

If you use Ashmere without registering, we do not collect any personally identifiable information. Anonymous usage data may still be collected as described in Section 1.4.

1.2 Registered Users

If you create an account, we collect the following:

  • Email address (if you register with email and password)
  • Name and email details provided by Google or Apple (if you register with Google Sign-In or Sign in with Apple). Apple may give us a private relay email address if you choose to hide your email.
  • A unique user identifier generated at account creation

We do not collect your phone number, physical address, or payment card details. Passwords are securely hashed and never stored in plain text.

1.3 Account-Linked Data

When you are signed in, the following is saved to your account and stored securely in our database:

  • Lesson progress and completion history
  • Streak, experience point (XP), rank, and badge data
  • Your profile (the display name, bio, and picture you choose)
  • App preferences and settings

This data is tied to your account so it can be restored if you reinstall the app or switch devices. It is not shared with third parties other than the service providers listed in Section 5.

1.4 Automatically Collected Information

Certain technical data may be collected when you use the app, unless you turn usage analytics off in Settings:

  • Device type, operating system version, and app version
  • In-app events, screens viewed, and interactions
  • Crash logs and performance diagnostics
  • General usage patterns and session duration

1.5 Advertising

Ashmere does not show advertising and does not use your data for advertising or tracking across other companies’ apps and websites.

1.6 Analytics Data

We use PostHog to understand how people use the app. For registered users, analytics events may be associated with your anonymous user ID but are never linked to your email address or real name. You can turn usage analytics off at any time in Settings.

1.7 Notifications

Reminders you turn on are scheduled on your device. We do not collect a push-notification token and do not send notifications from a server.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your optional account
  • Sync your progress, streaks, and preferences across devices
  • Operate, maintain, and improve Ashmere
  • Diagnose bugs, crashes, and technical issues
  • Understand which features and lessons people find useful
  • Manage subscriptions and free trials

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your data for any purpose beyond those listed above.

3. Data Storage and Security

Account data for registered users is stored using Supabase, a managed cloud database platform. Supabase provides encryption at rest and in transit. You can review Supabase’s security practices at: supabase.com/security

Passwords are never stored in plain text. Google Sign-In and Sign in with Apple are handled by Google and Apple; we only receive a secure token confirming your identity.

While we take reasonable technical measures to protect your data, no system is completely secure. If you register with email, we encourage you to use a strong, unique password.

4. Subscriptions

Ashmere offers the Scholar’s Pass, an auto-renewing subscription that may include a free trial. Purchases are processed by the Apple App Store on iPhone and by Google Play on Android. We do not handle your payment information directly. Subscriptions are managed through RevenueCat.

RevenueCat may collect limited data needed to validate and manage your subscription status. Review their privacy policy at: revenuecat.com/privacy

You can cancel at any time in your Apple Account settings (iPhone) or in Google Play (Android).

5. Third-Party Services

Ashmere integrates the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Sign-In — authentication
  • Sign in with Apple — authentication
  • PostHog — product analytics
  • Supabase — account data storage
  • RevenueCat — subscription management

6. Data Retention

Account data is retained for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal information and all account-linked data (progress, streaks, preferences) will be permanently deleted from our systems within 30 days.

Anonymized analytics data may be retained by PostHog under its own retention policy. Crash logs and diagnostics are typically retained for 90 days.

7. Your Rights and Choices

7.1 Use Without an Account

You may use Ashmere without registering. No account is required to try the app.

7.2 Access and Correction

If you have an account, you can review and update your profile information at any time in the app’s settings.

7.3 Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time in the app: open Settings, then Danger Zone. Deleting your account permanently removes your email address, profile, and all associated data from our systems within 30 days. You can also request deletion by email (see Section 10 and our account deletion page). Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in your Apple Account or Google Play settings first.

7.4 Analytics

You can turn off usage analytics at any time in Settings.

7.5 Data Portability and Other Rights

You can ask us for a copy of the data associated with your account, ask us to correct it, or object to how we use it. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have these rights under data protection law, including the right to complain to your local data protection authority. Contact us at the email below and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Children’s Privacy

Ashmere is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has created an account or provided personal data through the app, please contact us and we will remove it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this document. Significant changes will be communicated in the app. Continued use of Ashmere after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests about this Privacy Policy or your account data, please contact us:

We aim to respond to all privacy-related inquiries within 30 days.
